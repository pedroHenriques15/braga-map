# 082 · No photograph in the game: every texture made, matched to the photos

## Why

The owner's rule, 2026-10-23: **no photograph is used directly in the game**, a facade or anything
else. Photos stay as references, to measure, to judge colours and kinds, and for the scorecard.
Decision 002 allowed photo textures, and today the game draws three kinds of them, so what's
shipped breaks the rule until this is done.

## What

Today three kinds of photo texture reach the game, all made by `tools/textures.mjs` from
`refs/textures.json` into `world/textures/` (KTX2, ETC1S, decision 030):

- **10 facades:** reference photos (`refs/photos.json`) straightened onto their walls, drawn by
  party-kart's `src/render/photos.ts` in their building's colour (decision 036).
- **6 kinds of ground:** asphalt, paving, setts, grass, soil, gravel, each a Poly Haven photo
  (CC0), tiled by `src/render/cover.ts` over what `tools/cover.mjs` classes (decision 034).
- **9 kinds of tree:** each one's bark a Poly Haven photo, its leaves a spray composed from a Poly
  Haven tree's photos, drawn by `src/render/trees.ts` (decisions 043, 051).

The orthophoto isn't drawn since task 016 (only its colours are measured, e.g. a roof's), and
the landmarks and chapels are modelled "as their photos show", not textured with them: both are
already within the rule.

1. **The facades:** drop the ten photo facades from `refs/textures.json` and `world/textures/`;
   their buildings are drawn by the kit (decision 036), as the other 13,000 are. What each photo
   showed that the kit lacks (a tiled front's pattern, a shop's arcade) goes into that building's
   entry in `refs/buildings.json`, read from the photo as a reference.
2. **The ground:** each kind made, not photographed: a procedural material per kind (granite setts
   in Braga's sizes and joints, the calçada's limestone and basalt, asphalt's aggregate and wear,
   grass, soil, gravel), generated by a script into the same KTX2 files (or drawn in a shader in
   party-kart), its colour, scale and roughness matched to the reference photos of that kind in
   Braga (mean colour and contrast measured from `refs/photos.json`'s close-ups).
3. **The trees:** each kind's bark and leaves made: a bark generated from its kind's pattern
   (a plane's patches, a pine's plates, a eucalyptus's strips, a birch's white with dark
   lenticels), and leaves drawn from their shape (outline, veins, two tones, the stalk), laid
   along twigs as `refs/textures.json`'s `spray` already describes. Matched to their reference
   photos as in 2.
4. **The pipeline:** `tools/textures.mjs` keeps KTX2 and the per-level files; `refs/textures.json`
   records each texture's generator and parameters instead of a photo; `world/base.js`'s credit
   and party-kart's credits drop Poly Haven and the facades' photographers. A test fails if a
   texture's source is a photo (`test/textures.test.js` or the nearest test).
5. **Docs:** decision 002 and 030 replaced (a new DECISIONS.md entry when finished), the README's
   credits, `docs/SOURCES.md`'s Poly Haven entry (references only from now), party-kart's
   `photos.ts` renamed or removed and `docs/architecture.md`'s "Rendering".

## Notes

- The owner's answer (2026-10-23), in their words: "Nenhuma foto usada diretamente no jogo, seja
  fachada ou não. Pode-se usar imagens de referências e qualquer outro uso para melhorar." Decision
  018 (no new photos taken) stands.
- Priority `high`, not `critical`: the rule is new, nothing private is exposed. The owner may
  raise it.
- **How it went.** `tools/made.mjs` draws each texture from seeded noise and cells that wrap both
  ways (six grounds, nine barks, nine kinds of leaf); `textures.mjs --measure` reads each one's
  mean colour and contrast off its references (the Poly Haven textures that were drawn until now,
  and `closeup-pavement-setts-1` for the setts, the only Braga close-up fit to measure) into
  `refs/textures.json`, and each texture is brought to them. Decision 061.
- **The facades:** the ten photo facades are gone, `world.photos` with them (a breaking change for
  a game that read it; party-kart's `city.ts` doesn't any more). Five of their buildings had no
  entry in `refs/buildings.json`, or none seen: they have one now, read off their photos, and every
  one's notes say what the kit lacks of it. `npm run world` again: 652 guessed facades in the
  compared box changed with the shares of those seen, and two buildings' floors; nothing else.
- **The budget:** the textures' download on High 2.50 MB, was 3.29 (the facades' 0.78 MB at their
  three levels gone, the rest a little smaller); Medium 0.86, was 1.12; Low 0.26, was 0.32. The
  same sizes in pixels, ten fewer textures on the card. Each round's `stats.json` has the draw
  calls and frame times.
- **Two tags:** 1.33.0 drew the setts' joints so thin they vanished at Low; 1.33.1 widens them to
  1.6 cm, softens the granite's speckle and the concrete's stains, and puts fewer stones in the
  soil.
- **Not verified:** the owner's eye on any of it; the Medium level; a phone.
