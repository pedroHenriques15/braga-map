# 135 · The pollarded avenue trees: knuckled heads and short shoots, as Braga prunes them

## Why

Many of Braga's avenue trees are pollarded: cut back each winter to knuckled heads on a few thick
limbs, so in winter they stand as dark fists with short shoots, and in summer carry a compact crown
on them. The photos show it: `alameda-fujacal`'s (February) has the avenue bare and pollarded, and
`fujacal-west`'s tree is a pollarded head; task 061 found the Porfírio da Silva's white poplars and the
31 de Janeiro's west rows pollarded, and 088 the Alameda do Fujacal's. The game draws every tree of a
kind with the same free crown, so on Ultra in March (093, 132) the Fujacal's planes rise tall with
fine twigs where the photo has short, knuckled ones, and in leaf their crowns are wider than the
photos'. Found by worker-1 in 132's round (2026-10-25).

## What

1. Which trees are pollarded: from the photos (061's and 088's notes name rows) and the point cloud
   (a pollarded crown is short and compact over a tall clear trunk); a flag per tree, or per row, in
   the tiles if it needs one (a minor version).
2. Their shape on Ultra: a few thick limbs to knuckled heads, short shoots from them (bare in winter,
   a compact crown in leaf), from 089's limbs and 093's year; High as today's unless it's cheap.
3. Their bark: the photos of February show the Fujacal's trunks dark where the game's plane bark is
   pale; check whether that's the rain (a wet photo: 091's weather) or the trees' own bark, against a
   dry photo of the same row, and draw what holds.
4. Shoot `alameda-fujacal`, `fujacal-west`, `porfirio-da-silva` and `31-de-janeiro` at Ultra on 13 March,
   and the round.
